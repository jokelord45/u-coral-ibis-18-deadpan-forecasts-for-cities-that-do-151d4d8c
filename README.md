Entry for the [Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-ibis-18-deadpan-forecasts-for-cities-that-do-151d4d8c) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The task

Write a single line of at most 160 characters: a weather forecast for a city
that does not exist. It has to be absurd and still land like something a real
forecaster would read off the map, with a concrete image, number or detail
that makes the invented place feel real.

## The entry

> Overnight in Drizzlewick, a low of 3 degrees, a 90% chance of rain falling upwards, and light winds bringing the smell of somebody else's laundry from the east.

The rain falls upwards and the wind smells of somebody else's laundry — but
the numbers are exact, the tone is flat, and the forecaster does not blink.
Absurd weather, reported like ordinary weather.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt` (160 characters, one line).
